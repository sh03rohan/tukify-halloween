The Ritual

Six steps. No blood required. (Needs WooCommerce, PHP 7.4+.)

  1. Install and activate Tukify. WooCommerce must be active.
  2. Get an API key from your chosen provider. Gemini and Groq have free tiers.
  3. Open Tukify → Settings, paste the key, press Test connection.
  4. On the dashboard, click Reindex all products. It runs in the background.
  5. Enable the floating widget, or drop in the Elementor widgets (Chat, Search, Recommendations).
  6. Let it work. It never sleeps.

Whispers From the Dark

Is it really free?

Yes. The plugin is free. You connect your own AI key, and Gemini and Groq offer free tiers that are enough for many small stores.

Will it invent products?

No. Every answer is grounded in retrieval from your own catalog, and policy answers come only from your own content.

Do I need Elementor?

No. The floating chat widget works on any theme. Elementor is only for the three optional widgets.

Is my API key safe?

Keys are stored in your site options, shown masked in the admin, and used only server-side.

What happens if I delete it?

By default all its data is removed. Enable “Keep data” in Settings → Advanced to preserve it for a reinstall.
